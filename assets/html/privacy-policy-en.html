Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 2026

Durrat Alaseel is committed to protecting your personal data and privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when using our mobile application and services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect various types of information to provide and improve our services:

  • Account Data: Name, email address, phone number, and profile picture
  • Identity Data: ID verification documents and proof of address (when required)
  • Payment Information: Payment method details processed securely through third-party providers
  • Usage Data: Pages viewed, features used, interaction patterns, and app analytics
  • Device Data: Device type, OS version, IP address, and unique device identifiers
  • Location Data: GPS location information (only with your explicit permission)
  • Communication Data: Messages, feedback, and support tickets you send us

2. How We Use Your Information

We process your personal data for the following purposes:

  • To create and manage your account and provide access to our services
  • To process transactions and payments securely
  • To send notifications, updates, and service communications
  • To improve user experience and optimize app functionality
  • To conduct fraud prevention and security measures
  • To respond to your inquiries and provide customer support
  • To analyze usage patterns and app performance
  • To comply with legal obligations and regulatory requirements

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your data with:

  • Service Providers: Payment processors, cloud hosting providers, analytics services
  • Business Partners: With your explicit consent for specific partnerships
  • Legal Authorities: When required by law or to protect our rights
  • Successor Organizations: In case of merger, acquisition, or sale of assets

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information:

  • End-to-end encryption for sensitive data transmission
  • Secure servers with firewall protection
  • Regular security audits and vulnerability assessments
  • Access controls limiting employee access to personal data
  • Secure password storage and authentication mechanisms

However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your data.

5. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your location, you have the following rights:

  • Access: Request copies of your personal data
  • Correction: Request inaccurate or incomplete data be corrected
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your data (subject to legal requirements)
  • Portability: Request your data in a portable format
  • Opt-out: Opt-out of marketing communications at any time
  • Withdraw Consent: Withdraw consent for data processing

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to provide our services and fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy. When you request account deletion, we will remove your information within 30 days, except where we are required by law to retain it.

7. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies (tokens, local storage) to:

  • Enhance your user experience and remember your preferences
  • Analyze app usage and performance
  • Provide personalized content and recommendations
  • Prevent fraud and maintain app security

8. Children's Privacy

Our services are not intended for users under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If we become aware that a child has provided us with personal information, we will take steps to delete such information promptly.
